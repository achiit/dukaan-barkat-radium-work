MA

Mohammed Ameer, owner

My uncle ran a small radium counter in Musheerabad. I used to sweep the cut-off vinyl as a boy and sort the colours by the roll. In 2009 I opened Barkat Radium Work near Anjuman Boys High School with one cutting machine and a rack of sample stickers.

Most of our customers are neighbours. Boys from the school bring their bike plates, shop owners in Bholakpur bring their names, and families bring house numbers before a function. I still remember the first number plate I made for a Royal Enfield. The owner came back for his friend's plate the next week.

Bring a rough idea. I will not start cutting till you have seen the layout and agreed to it.

  • You see the layout before we cut
  • Sample wall with hundreds of designs
  • Rates told upfront, no add-ons at the counter
  • Letters in Urdu, Hindi, Telugu and English
  • Redo free if the spelling is our mistake
